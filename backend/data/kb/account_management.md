# Account Management

## Changing the registered mobile number
The registered mobile number can be changed under Profile > Edit Profile. An OTP is sent to both the old and the new number for verification. If the old number is no longer active, the student needs to contact support with their order ID for manual verification.

## Changing email or name
The email address and name on the account can be updated anytime under Profile > Edit Profile.

## Sharing an account
An EduPrime account is meant for one student only. Accounts found being shared across many devices or people may be temporarily blocked.

## Account deletion
Students can request account deletion under Profile > Settings > Delete Account. The account and its data are permanently deleted after 30 days, and purchased batches cannot be restored after deletion.
