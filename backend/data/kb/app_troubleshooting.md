# App and Technical Troubleshooting

## Videos buffering or not playing
If lectures keep buffering, the student should lower the video quality from the player settings, switch to a stable Wi-Fi network, and update the app to the latest version. Clearing the app cache from phone Settings > Apps > EduPrime > Storage > Clear Cache also fixes most playback issues.

## Supported devices
The EduPrime app works on Android 9 and above and on iOS 15 and above. Lectures can also be watched on the website using the latest version of Chrome, Edge or Safari.

## OTP not received during login
If the login OTP does not arrive, the student should wait 60 seconds before requesting a new one and check that DND or SMS blocking is not enabled. Students can also choose the "Get OTP on email" option on the login screen.

## Device limit
An account can be logged in on a maximum of 2 devices at the same time. To log in on a new device, the student should log out from an older device under Profile > Settings > Manage Devices.

## App crashing or not opening
If the app crashes, the student should update it to the latest version, clear the cache, and restart the phone. If the problem continues, reinstalling the app usually fixes it, but offline downloads will be deleted and need to be downloaded again.

## Reporting a bug
If none of the steps work, the student should share their phone model, Android or iOS version, app version and a screen recording of the issue with support so the Tech Support team can investigate.
