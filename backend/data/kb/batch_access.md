# Batch Access

## Activation after purchase
A batch is usually activated instantly after a successful payment, and in rare cases it can take up to 2 hours. The batch appears under My Batches on the app and the website.

## Batch not visible after purchase
If a purchased batch is not visible, the student should first log out and log back in, and make sure they are logged in with the same mobile number used for the purchase. If the batch is still missing after 2 hours of a successful payment, the case needs to be checked by the support team using the order ID.

## Changing to a different batch
A student can switch to a different batch once, within 15 days of purchase, provided the new batch is of the same or higher price. If the new batch costs more, the student pays the difference. Batch change requests are processed by the Academic Operations team within 48 hours.

## Joining a batch that has already started
Students who join a batch after it has started get access to all recorded lectures from the beginning of the batch, so no content is missed.

## Batch validity
Each batch is valid until the date shown on the batch page, which is usually the end of the target exam cycle. After validity ends, lectures, notes and downloads are no longer accessible.

## Validity extension
A one-time validity extension of 3 months can be purchased for Rs 499 from the batch page, up to 30 days after the batch expires.
