# Doubt Support

## How to ask an academic doubt
Academic doubts about a concept, question or solution should be asked through the "Ask Doubt" button below any lecture or in the Doubts section of the batch. Students can attach a photo of the question.

## Doubt response time
Faculty and subject mentors reply to doubts within 24 hours. The AI Doubt Assistant in the Doubts section gives an instant first answer, which mentors review.

## Doubts sent to the support team
The customer support team does not solve academic questions. Academic doubts received by support are forwarded to the subject mentor team, and the student is guided to use the Doubts section for faster answers.
