# Offline Downloads

## How downloads work
Lectures can be downloaded for offline viewing inside the EduPrime mobile app only. Downloaded videos can only be played inside the app and cannot be copied or shared.

## Download limits
A maximum of 100 lectures can be stored offline at a time. Downloads are not available on the website.

## Download expiry
Downloaded lectures stay available until the batch validity ends. They are also removed if the app is uninstalled or the student logs out.
