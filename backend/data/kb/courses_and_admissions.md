# Courses and Admissions

## Exams and courses offered
EduPrime offers online batches for JEE, NEET, UPSC, GATE (CSE, ECE, EE, ME and CE), CAT, and school students from Class 6 to Class 12. Each exam has separate batches for each target year, for example GATE 2027 batches for students appearing in the 2027 exam.

## Finding batches open for enrolment
Every batch currently open for enrolment is listed in the Explore section of the app and on the Batches page of the website, along with its start date, price, faculty and syllabus. If a batch is not listed in Explore, it is closed for enrolment.

## Free demo lectures
Every batch page has free demo lectures that can be watched before buying, so students can check the teaching style first.

## Free counselling call
Students who are unsure which batch to choose can book a free call with an academic counsellor by tapping "Talk to a Counsellor" in the Explore section. A counsellor calls back within 24 hours.

## Scholarships and discounts
EduPrime holds a free scholarship test every month that gives discounts of up to 50% on batch fees. The date of the next scholarship test and any current offers are shown on the Explore page.
