# Refund Policy

## Eligibility for a full refund
A full refund is available if the request is raised within 7 days of purchase and the student has watched fewer than 3 hours of lectures in the batch. Requests raised after 7 days from the date of purchase are not eligible for a refund, except for duplicate payments or a confirmed technical failure on EduPrime's side.

## How to request a refund
Students can request a refund from the app under Profile > My Purchases > Request Refund, or by writing to support with their registered mobile number and order ID. Every refund request is reviewed and approved by the Billing team; the support assistant cannot approve refunds on its own.

## Refund timelines
Once a refund is approved, the amount is credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Refunds cannot be transferred to a different bank account, UPI ID or card than the one used for payment.

## Deductions for books and study material
If printed books or study material have already been shipped for the batch, a deduction of Rs 300 is made from the refund amount to cover shipping. Books do not need to be returned.

## Refunds on EMI purchases
For batches bought on EMI, the refund is processed to the card or EMI partner, and the student may need to contact their bank to close the EMI plan. Any interest or processing fee charged by the bank is not refundable by EduPrime.
