# Support Hours and Escalations

## Support hours and response time
The EduPrime support team is available from 9 AM to 9 PM, all seven days. Most queries get a response within 24 hours.

## Escalated cases
Cases that need action by the Billing, Academic Operations or Tech Support teams are escalated, and the student receives an update within 48 hours.

## Complaints and grievances
Students who are not satisfied with a resolution can ask for their case to be reviewed by a senior support manager. Every complaint receives a ticket number for tracking.
