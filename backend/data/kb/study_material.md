# Books and Study Material

## Dispatch and delivery
Printed books included in a batch are dispatched within 7 working days of purchase and are usually delivered within 5 to 10 working days after dispatch, depending on the location.

## Tracking an order
A tracking link is sent by SMS and email once the books are dispatched. The tracking link is also available under Profile > My Orders.

## Damaged or missing books
If books arrive damaged or a book is missing, the student must share photos of the package and the books with support within 48 hours of delivery. A replacement is then dispatched free of charge.

## Address change
The delivery address can be changed under Profile > My Orders only before the books are dispatched.
