# Payment Issues

## Money debited but payment failed
If money was debited but the app shows the payment as failed, the bank automatically reverses the amount within 5 to 7 working days. No action is needed from the student during this period. If the amount is not reversed after 7 working days, the student should share the transaction ID (UTR number) and a screenshot of the debit with support so the Billing team can trace it.

## Money debited but batch not activated
If the payment was successful but the batch is not visible after 2 hours, the student should share the order ID or UTR number with support. These cases are escalated to the Billing team, who manually activate the batch after verifying the payment.

## Duplicate payments
If a student is charged twice for the same batch, the extra payment is identified automatically and refunded to the original payment method within 7 working days.

## Accepted payment methods
EduPrime accepts UPI, debit cards, credit cards, net banking and popular wallets. Cash payments are only accepted at EduPrime offline centres.

## No-cost EMI
No-cost EMI is available on credit cards and select debit cards for purchases above Rs 3,000. EMI eligibility and limits are decided by the bank or EMI partner, not by EduPrime. If an EMI transaction is declined, the student should check their card's EMI eligibility with the bank or choose another payment method.

## Invoices
The GST invoice for every purchase can be downloaded from Profile > My Purchases > Download Invoice. All batch prices shown in the app are inclusive of GST.
